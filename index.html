Healthcare Operations Management

My research examines healthcare service systems in which capacity constraints, patient behavior, and institutional incentives jointly shape operational decisions and system design.

About Me

I am currently an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Zhengzhou University. I received my Ph.D. in Business Administration from the School of Economics and Management, Tongji University. My doctoral advisor is Professor Chunyang Tong. During my Ph.D. study, I was a joint-training doctoral student at the Sauder School of Business, University of British Columbia, advised by Professor Mahesh Nagarajan.

My research focuses on healthcare operations management, healthcare data analytics, and data-driven optimization. I am particularly interested in the design, operational control, and policy optimization of healthcare service systems with capacity constraints and complex patient behavior. My research settings include tiered healthcare systems, healthcare payment reform, and AI-assisted triage. Methodologically, I use stochastic modeling, optimization theory, machine learning, and causal inference.

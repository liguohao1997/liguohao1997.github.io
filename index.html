About

Guohao Li is an Assistant Professor / Lecturer at the School of Management, Zhengzhou University. He received his Ph.D. in Business Administration from Tongji University and was a visiting doctoral student in the Operations and Logistics Division at the Sauder School of Business, University of British Columbia.

His research focuses on healthcare operations management, smart healthcare operations, telemedicine, data-driven operations, and behavioral operations. His current work examines tiered healthcare systems, patient choice, referral design, healthcare payment reform, medical resource allocation, and the operational implications of machine learning and causal inference in service systems.

Research Interests

Healthcare Service System Design

Tiered healthcare systems, referral design, gatekeeping, patient choice, access governance, and capacity allocation.

Smart Healthcare Operations

Telemedicine, AI-assisted triage, medical resource scheduling, and data-enabled healthcare delivery.

Payment Reform and Operational Response

DRG/DIP payment constraints, hospital operational adaptation, quality-risk trade-offs, and decision support.

Methods

Optimization, game theory, queueing models, machine learning, causal inference, Python, SQL, MATLAB, and LaTeX.
